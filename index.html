ABOUT US

About Our College

Founded in 1946 by Bharat Ratna Dr. B. R. Ambedkar under the People’s Education Society, Siddharth College of Arts, Science & Commerce is a landmark educational institution located in the heart of Fort, Mumbai. Built on the principle of making higher education accessible to all sections of society, the college combines academic tradition with modern learning. We are committed to fostering critical thinking, technical proficiency, and holistic student development.

✦

Our Vision

To promote intellectual, moral, and social democracy through education, empowering students to innovate, lead, and contribute meaningfully to modern India.

Our Community

Our Achievements

THE MAIN EVENT

About the Event

ZEAL is the flagship annual inter-collegiate IT festival hosted by the Department of Information Technology at Siddharth College. Step into a realm where technology crosses dimensions—this year, ZEAL presents "Multiverse," an extraordinary platform where parallel realms of logic, creativity, and digital execution converge. Built around the tagline "Where Ideas Meet Opportunity," ZEAL invites students from both IT and non-IT backgrounds across colleges to showcase their talent, test their technical instincts, and collaborate across diverse tracks. From multi-dimensional coding grids and PR crisis simulations to creative digital showcases, ZEAL offers a dynamic space to learn, compete, and build lasting connections across the tech universe. Key Focus Areas: Learning: Hands-on experience with modern technical paradigms and industry-aligned challenges. Creativity: Original thinking through multi-faceted design, strategy, and problem-solving simulations. Teamwork: Collaborative execution, cross-college networking, and competitive leadership. Innovation: Spotlighting fresh ideas, technical projects, and smart execution strategies across parallel disciplines.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in the event?

ZEAL is an inter-collegiate event open to all junior college and undergraduate students. Valid college ID cards are mandatory during entry and event registration verification.

How do I register?

Registrations can be completed online via our official festival portal or registration links provided on our social media handles. Spot registrations may also be available at the venue subject to event capacity and slot availability.

Where will the event take place?

ZEAL will be hosted on-campus at Siddharth College. Specific room allocations, labs, and auditorium venues for individual technical and non-technical competitions will be signposted at the registration desk.

How can I contact the organizers?

You can reach out to the student coordinators and event leads directly through our official festival helpline numbers, via WhatsApp at the contact section.
