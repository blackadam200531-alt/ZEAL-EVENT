About the Game

A short-form content creation event where participants shoot, edit, and produce a creative video on campus.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • Recommended duration: 30–60 seconds in vertical 9:16 format. The organiser must announce the theme, deadline, file format, and submission method.
  • The reel must be created by the registered participant/team. Do not download and resubmit another creator’s reel.
  • Use original footage or media that you have permission to use. Use licensed, royalty-free, or platform-approved audio; give credit where required.
  • Obtain consent before recording identifiable people. Do not film in restricted areas, disrupt classes, or record private conversations.
