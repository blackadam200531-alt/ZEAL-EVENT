About the Game

A solo performance event where participants deliver original comedic routines and jokes live on stage.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • Recommended stage time: 3–5 minutes per performer, plus a short changeover. A warning may be given when 30 seconds remain.
  • Content must be original or appropriately credited. Do not present another comedian’s routine as your own.
  • No hate speech, bullying, targeted humiliation, threats, explicit sexual content, discriminatory jokes, or jokes that reveal someone’s private information.
  • Props, music, or audience interaction require prior approval. Do not use dangerous props or interrupt another performer.
