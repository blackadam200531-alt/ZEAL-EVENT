About the Game

A tabletop strike-and-pocket game where players flick a heavy striker to pocket wooden carrom men and the red queen.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • The event is played in a 1 vs 1 format.
  • One player must pocket only black coins, while the other pockets only white coins.
  • The queen is included and must be covered by the same player who pockets it.
  • The participant who pockets all of their assigned coins first is declared the winner.
  • Only proper striking rules and fair gameplay will be considered.
