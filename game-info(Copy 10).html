About the Game

A mystery-solving game where participants investigate technology-related cases by analysing clues, solving puzzles and identifying the correct answers.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • Each case may contain screenshots, logs, clues, diagrams, or short scenarios. All material will be fictional or suitably anonymised.
  • Participants must identify the likely issue, select supporting evidence, and write a short explanation or recommended safe response.
  • No participant may access real accounts, devices, networks, or private data. This is a paper/simulation challenge only.
  • Do not attempt to scan, test, access, or interfere with college systems or another person's device.
