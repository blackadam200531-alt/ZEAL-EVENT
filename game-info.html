A multiplayer battle royale mobile game where players drop onto an island, search for weapons and supplies, and fight to be the last team standing.

Gaming Rules

  • The tournament is strictly for squads consisting of 4 members.
  • All players must use mobile devices only.
  • Teams must carry their own power banks and earphones.
  • No hacks, cheats, or third-party tools are allowed. Any violation will result in direct disqualification.
  • The use of foul language is strictly prohibited.
  • An in-game account ID level of 25 or above is required.
  • The organizers' decisions are final and binding. Organizers are not responsible for ping issues, lag, or glitches.
