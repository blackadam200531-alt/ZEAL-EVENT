About the Game

A challenge where participants write and refine prompts for generative AI models to create accurate text, code, or visual outputs.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • The organiser will announce the task, approved AI tool(s), time limit, allowed number of attempts, and whether participants may use their own devices.
  • Participants must write their own prompts. Do not copy another participant’s prompt or use a pre-prepared prompt unless the rules explicitly permit it.
  • Do not enter personal, confidential, sensitive, or identifying information into an AI tool. Use only the fictional/sample material supplied for the event.
  • Do not use prompts to generate hateful, explicit, deceptive, or harmful content. All outputs must be suitable for a college audience.
