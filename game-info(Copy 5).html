About the Game

A outdoor team sport where two teams attempt to score points by maneuvering a ball into the opposing goal.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • Recommended squad: Up to 8 registered players, with 5 on the field, including the goalkeeper. Substitutions are rolling and must be made at the designated area.
  • Recommended match duration: Two halves of 7–10 minutes with a short interval. The exact duration, ball size, ground boundaries, and goal dimensions must be announced.
  • Kick-off and team ends are decided by toss. The ball is out when it fully crosses a boundary line. The referee restarts play using the method announced for the venue.
  • No slide tackles, pushing, tripping, dangerous contact, deliberate handball (except by the goalkeeper in the permitted area), or abusive conduct.
