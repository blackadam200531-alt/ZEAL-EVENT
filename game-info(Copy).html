About the Game

A team adventure game where participants solve cryptic clues and follow markers to locate hidden checkpoints.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • Recommended team size: 3–5 participants. All members must be registered and remain together unless a clue specifically permits otherwise.
  • The organiser will explain the boundaries, start/finish points, time limit, and how to request a hint.
  • Teams must solve clues in order and record answers or collect authorised tokens at each checkpoint.
  • Do not enter locked rooms, staff-only areas, laboratories without permission, rooftops, roads, or restricted zones. Do not climb, run on stairs, move furniture, or disturb classes.
