About the Game

A timed keyboarding test measuring a participant's words per minute and typing accuracy.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • 1 minute for a qualifying round and 3 minutes for the final. The organiser must announce the exact duration and website/software.
  • Participants must type the displayed passage exactly. Do not paste text, use voice typing, predictive text, macros, or external assistance.
  • The organiser should use the same passage for all participants in a round, or passages of equivalent length and difficulty.
  • Participants may not change keyboard, language, font size, or test settings after the test starts unless the coordinator permits it.
