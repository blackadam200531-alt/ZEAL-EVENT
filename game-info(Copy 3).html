About the Game

A classic two-player strategy board game played on a 64-square grid with the goal of checkmating the opponent's king.

PLAY FAIR

Gaming Rules

  • When castling, a player must first move the king with one hand and then move the rook with the same hand.
  • The movement of all pieces must be executed with one hand.
  • For promotions, if a player releases a pawn on the eighth rank, they must promote it.
  • After a pawn has moved, the player may touch a piece not on the board, but the promotion is only finalized once the new piece is released on the promotion square.
  • If a player touches an opponent's piece, that piece must be captured if there is a legal move to do so. If no legal move or capture is possible, there is no penalty.
